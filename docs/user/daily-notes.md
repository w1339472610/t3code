# Daily notes

Daily notes are a scratch outline per day: things to remember, prompts you will send later, and the threads you are working on. Open them from **Daily notes** at the top of the sidebar, the command palette, or `Mod+Shift+U`. To keep them beside a thread, open **Daily notes** from the right panel's **+** menu.

Switch between **Day**, **Week**, and **Month** at the top. Day shows a single day to focus on. Week and Month lay out every day of the period on one screen, and every day stays editable there; click a day's date to open it on its own. The arrows step to the previous or next period, **Today** comes back, and the calendar button jumps to any day, including future ones. In a narrow window or the right panel, the days of a week or month stack instead, and Month lists only the days that have notes.

- `Enter` starts a new line, `Shift+Enter` breaks the line inside a note, `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` indent and outdent. Click the empty space below a day's notes to add a line at the end.
- `Mod+Enter` cycles a note between plain text, to-do, and done. Typing `[] ` at the start of a note also makes it a to-do, and `[x] ` makes it done.
- `Mod+↑` and `Mod+↓` fold and unfold a note's children; `Mod+Shift+↑` and `Mod+Shift+↓` move it.
- `Esc` selects the note; so does dragging the pointer from one note into others, or `Shift`-clicking another note. Each further `Mod+A` widens the selection to the enclosing note, then to the whole day. With notes selected, `Shift+↑`/`Shift+↓` extend the selection, and you can copy, cut, paste over, delete, indent, or turn them into to-dos.
- Drag a note's bullet to move it with its children, within its day or onto another day in Week or Month.
- Type `@` to mention a thread. Mentions show the thread's live title and status; click one to open it. You can also drag threads from the sidebar between two notes.
- Select a single note and choose **Start a thread** to open a new thread with the note as its message. Once you send it, the note becomes a mention of the new thread and its children fold under it.

Each day is a Markdown file named `YYYY-MM-DD.md`, kept by the machine you are connected to, in the `notes` folder of T3 Code's data folder. Change the folder in **Settings → General → Daily notes**, for example to a folder in an Obsidian vault. Edits made to the files elsewhere, by you or by an agent, show up in T3 Code while the day is not being edited.
